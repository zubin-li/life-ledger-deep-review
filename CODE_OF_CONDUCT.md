# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, patient, and specific.
- Respect that journaling, health habits, and personal data can be sensitive.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person.
- Remove private information from examples, screenshots, and bug reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate exposure of private data, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Serious security or privacy concerns should be reported privately according to `SECURITY.md`.
